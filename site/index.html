ihz.land

A living island at the top of your screen.

Music with lyrics in time, your day at a glance, a shelf for files, a focus timer, prayer times and an IELTS speaking coach. It sits where you look, and stays out of the way.

Download for Windows Source on GitHub

Version 0.2.30 · Windows 10 and 11 · no admin rights needed

What's on the island

Music

The song from any player, with synced lyrics and readings for Japanese, Korean and Chinese.

Today

The date, five weeks at a glance, and how your PC is doing.

Shelf and phone drop

Park files on the island and send them to or from your phone.

Tasks and focus

A short list for the day and a focus timer that counts your sessions.

Prayer times

Today's times for your city, with a reminder and the adzan.

IELTS coach

Pulo asks IELTS Speaking questions out loud, listens and gives you a band, with Teman IELTS.

AI usage

What's left of your AI coding plans, read on your own computer.

Downloader

Paste a link and keep its video or sound.

Pulo

An optional theme where Pulo, a small island with a sprout, lives in the island.

Light by design

Nothing runs at rest unless you switched it on. Add-ons download only when you turn them on, and switching one off deletes its files. Signing in to ihz.app is optional and only needed for the IELTS coach.

ihz.land is a personal fork of Oyenza Studio, whose code is MIT licensed. The Oyenza name, logo and the Oyen character belong to that project and aren't used here.
