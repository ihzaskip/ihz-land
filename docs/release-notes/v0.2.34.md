**Copy text from anywhere on the screen, and record the screen without another app.**

- **Text extractor:** press **Alt+Shift+T** in any app (or choose **Extract text** in the menu), drag a box over some text and it's on the clipboard. Windows' own text recognition reads it, in the languages Windows has, so nothing is downloaded. Switch on **Text extractor** with "+" for a Text tab: an Extract text button, the shortcut, and your last five texts, a click copying one again.
- **Screen recorder:** press **Alt+Shift+R** (or choose **Record screen** in the menu) to record the screen the island is on, with the PC's sound and your microphone, to an MP4 in Videos. Switch on **Screen recorder** with "+" for a Record tab with Mic and Sound switches, Record and Stop. While it records, the closed island shows a beating red dot and the time; a click on it stops and saves the video. The island stays out of the video.
- **Record microphone** in the menu chooses whether the microphone goes in.
