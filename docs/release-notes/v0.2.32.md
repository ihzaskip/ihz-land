**Quran, a math quiz and lyrics you can fix by hand.**

- **Quran:** Prayer's "Read Quran" now opens a Quran page in the island instead of the browser. One ayah at a time in large Arabic, the word being recited lit up, the Kemenag translation underneath and Mishary Alafasy's recitation. The text and its font are a 1.3 MB add-on, downloaded the first time you open it; the recitation streams only while it plays.
- **Kuis:** JISPA math questions from ihz.app, one at a time, on a Kuis tab you open yourself. Switch it on with "+". It needs the ihz.app sign-in, like the IELTS coach. Windows only for now.
- **Lyrics:** when the Music page finds the wrong lyrics, or none, pick the right ones from LRCLIB's search yourself, or choose "Tanpa lirik". Your choice stays with that song.
- **Lyrics:** songs titled 'Artist feat. X - Song' are found again.
