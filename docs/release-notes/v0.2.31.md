**The first ihz.land release.** ihz.land is a personal fork of Oyenza Studio 0.2.30 (MIT), with its own name, its own character, Pulo, and its own update channel.

- **Pulo** replaces the original mascot on the island, the icon and the installer.
- **The account features are gone:** the Oyenza tab, Health, Stocks, Meeting notes, Today's agenda, Oyenza Inference and Studio Pro. What stays is the optional sign-in to **ihz.app**, which only the IELTS coach uses.
- **IELTS coach:** Pulo asks Speaking questions out loud and gives you a band with feedback in Sopan, Gaul or Ngegas, through [Teman IELTS](https://temanielts.com).
- **Updates** come from this fork's own signed feed. When the original project releases, the island says so.

Your data stays where it was (`%APPDATA%\Poni\`), so installing over Oyenza Studio keeps your settings.
