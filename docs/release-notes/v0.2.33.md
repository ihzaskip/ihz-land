**Follow Quran recitation on the closed island and jump to any surah.**

- While a recitation is playing, the closed island shows the current ayah when **Show what's playing** is enabled. The highlight follows the recited word, and longer ayat scroll to keep that word visible.
- Open **Choose surah** in the Quran tab to browse all 114 surahs or search by name or number. Choosing a surah starts at its first ayah.
- The Quran add-on supplies the text and timing. The recitation display updates while visible and playing, and rests during battery saver.
